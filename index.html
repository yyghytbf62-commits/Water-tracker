Confirming a leak
  • Turn off every tap and appliance that uses water, then note the meter. Check again after 1–2 hours without using any water. Any change suggests a leak.
  • Many meters have a small spinning dial or triangle that moves when any water flows, even a slow drip.
  • Common culprits: running toilet cisterns, dripping taps, irrigation, and underground pipe leaks.
  • Take readings at about the same time of day for the most consistent results.
